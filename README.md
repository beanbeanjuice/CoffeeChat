# CoffeeChat

## What is CoffeeChat?

Despite what the name might imply, it's not a social media or messaging platform. CoffeeChat is simply a safe, secure way to share your calendar with people online in the browser.

Right now, it's in a very rudimentary state, and I honestly don't plan to update it much further than this.

## How do I use CoffeeChat?

Just take a look at the [docker-compose.yml](./docker-compose.yml).
